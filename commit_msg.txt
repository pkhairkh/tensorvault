feat(w2): parallel morsel-driven join probe

Refactor hash_join_with_keys probe phase to use rayon parallel morsels.
The build phase (hash table + bloom filter) stays serial. The probe
phase splits the probe side into 65536-row chunks, each thread probes
independently and produces its own output columns. Results are merged
by concatenation at the end.

The build_hash (JoinHashTable) and bloom (BloomFilter) are shared
read-only across threads. Each thread gets its own output buffers to
avoid contention.

This is the biggest performance win for join-heavy queries.
